## Submit Change Detection
The **Submit Change Detection** script is designed to facilitate the generation of Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC) images or results using Landsat satellite imagery from the period of 2010 to 2024. This script works by integrating spectral bands and coefficient , enabling the detection of land cover changes over time. 

## Format Training Data
The **Format Training Data** script plays a crucial role in preparing training datasets for change detection and classification. This script enhances the training samples by incorporating **spectral bands** and **coefficients**, ensuring that the training data aligns with the structure of the **Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC)** results or images. By adding spectral bands, the script enriches the dataset with relevant spectral information from satellite imagery, improving the accuracy of classification models. Additionally, the inclusion of coefficients helps in refining the spectral-temporal features, enabling a more precise representation of land cover changes over time. This process is essential for ensuring that the raw training data is compatible with the spectral characteristics of the **CCDC outputs**, leading to more reliable and meaningful analysis in applications such as **land use monitoring, deforestation assessment, urban expansion tracking, and environmental management**.

## Classify and Map Change
The **Classify and Map Change** process facilitates the generation of land use and land cover (LULC) classifications by integrating CCDC images or results with training samples that contain spectral bands. At this stage, the classification process assigns land cover types based on spectral characteristics, ensuring accurate representation of changes over time. Additionally, the color palette for different land cover classes is defined, and the year for classification is specified, allowing for clear visualization and interpretation of the results.
